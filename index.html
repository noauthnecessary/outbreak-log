How to read the tags. Each entry makes three separate claims, tagged separately. Event: shown in-game (you see it happen), from dialogue (a character or file says it happened), or reconstructed (my juxtaposition across the two games). Time: date confirmed (the game states the date), relative timing (follows from something stated, such as "24 hours before RE2" or "half a day later"), or clock estimated (only the story order is known). Place: numbered on map, geographically inferred (not on the map, but the game ties it to something that is, such as NEST 2 under the hospital), or speculative. Solid tags are the strongest, outlined tags are inferences, dashed tags are guesses. Even "date confirmed" entries have estimated clock hours.

Trails are dashed lines joining the places a character has already visited, in order. They show sequence only, not the streets anyone actually took. Markers jump from stop to stop for the same reason.

The map is "Map of Raccoon City – Home of Umbrella" by enetirnel on DeviantArt, cropped to the city. Background art: Resident Evil 2 and Resident Evil 3 key art © Capcom. This is an unofficial fan project. It is not an official Capcom map, and its author has said parts of it are off. RE2 is shown as one combined night, and only Claire's route goes through the orphanage.
